Stuff about bunnies!

basics about bunnies:

  • They are cute
  • they are fluffy
  • they come in different colors

Bunnies are very cute prey mammals that are domesticatable. When they aren't domesticated, they can be called hares or rabbits. They eat a variety of plants, like:

  1. carrots
  2. cabbage
  3. leafy greens
  4. pellets

I really want a bunny because They are one of my favorite animals but my parents won't allow it. They have short tempers and will get "mad" at their owners by stomping their feet. They can also show when their comfortable with you by flopping down on their stomachs. Bunnies have long big ears so they can hear predators coming in advance, this does make them quite skiddish animals. overall, they are good pets and fantastic animals that should be appreciated more.
