The start of it all

music is something that multiple people can connect through and understand, but most people don't exactly know where it came from. music seemed to sprout through mythology, religion, and philosophical beliefs. in 38,000 BC during paleolithic times, it is shown that people invented bone flutes, being exactly what it sounds like. In these early times, people only used percussion instruments and flutes. many cultures helped the growth of music such as, egyptian, indian, greek, mesopotamian, and other ancient societies. When the silk road came to be, multiple cultures spread their ideas of musical science and instruments. Religion was a big part in helping music spread and grow througout history. Even though many people believe music was born at some point in 38,000 BC, it is hard to statistically track down the exact time and place, for historians are just guesstimating.

Birth of instruments

instruments we will be talking about:

  • flute
  • piano
  • guitar
  • drums
  • orchestral instruments
  • odd instruments

The oldest recorded instrument in history is a neanderthal flute, being 60,000 years old. It was made out of a thighbone of a paleolithic bear, holes were drilled into it using a pointed stone. for the next couple 100,000 years different variants of flutes were being made. An ancient instrument called a lithophone, originating from india and vietnam, was made with resonant rocks and looks like a crappy combination of a glockenspiel and a piano. When the egypt civilization came to be, king tut had early made trumpets being about 3,340 years old and made from silver, bronze and/or copper. These inventions have helped shape modern and sleek instruments that we have today.
